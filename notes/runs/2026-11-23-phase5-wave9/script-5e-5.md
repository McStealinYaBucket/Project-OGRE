## Patterns proven (for CLAUDE.md, the integrator's call)

- **2013 `Type::operator==`/`!=` are out of line with asserts** (Type.h 41/45; PLAYER 0x00447390 has 200
  callers). Every 2013 function that compares two Types calls it. **There is no `Type::isEnum`.**
  `isFloat`/`isNumber` come from `boost::is_float`/`is_arithmetic` (`Type<bool>` 0x006fdf30).
- **`boost::array<T,N>::assign(v)` is the tell for `xor eax,eax; mov ecx,eax; mov edx,eax`.** Aggregate
  `{{0,0}}`, chained `a[0]=a[1]=0` and value-init all zero one register (closeStates 0x0068540c).
  **`std::swap(a, b)`**: VC9 loads the tmp from `a` and its self-check is `cmp &a,&b`.
- **VC9 tail-merges identical stores** in `if (x) s; else if (y) s;`. The 2013 shape is two branches
  into one store block, not an `||` (delay 0x0067a3b0).
- **An inline header helper can be out of line at one call site and inlined at another in the same
  function** (`Lua::lua_pushstring(L, std::string)`, resumeThreadWithException 0x00879980). When the
  helper is inlined, the caller's argument (L) is evaluated before the body's `c_str()`/`size()`.
  That order tells you a helper was used where a hand-spelled `lua_pushlstring` would compute c_str first.
