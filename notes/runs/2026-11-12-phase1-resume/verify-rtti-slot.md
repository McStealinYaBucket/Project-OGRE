## Systematic causes

- No rule in pair_names.py's rtti-slot pass produced a wrong pairing that I could show. I sampled 60 rows with seed 20261112 and decompiled both sides with Hex-Rays on scratch copies of PLAYER, MACSTUDIO and IOS_1029: tools/rtti_slot_sample.py, then tools/ida/decomp_dump.py. One row per address, in five strata: overload 15 of 28, mi (secondary vftable) 15 of 19, tail (vftable of 40+ slots, one of its last 8 slots) 15 of 92, folded 5 of 326, random 10 of 2640. All 60 match on assert strings and line numbers, FastLog strings, callee order, switch cases, vcall offsets (MSVC offset = Itanium offset minus 4 after the destructor, as at 0x006d2920 +12 vs +20 and 0x00769ed0 +132/+160 vs +148/+180) and stack arity. 11 of the 60 are trivial bodies (ret, ret N, return 0/1/this, one-line thunks); there only arity and the shape of the constant could be checked. 6 extra iOS-only rows (0x00490990 0x00505150 0x004b81d0 0x00490960 0x004a3ce0 0x0051f180) also match. No Mac-only single-name row uses the iOS-guided layout, so none could be drawn.
- Population-wide arity test (tools/rtti_slot_arity_check.py): it compares each name's Itanium parameter words with the `ret N` in the PLAYER body (thiscall; +4 allowed for a hidden return pointer). It checked 7,489 of the 7,996 rtti-slot rows and flagged 0 (both 0 of 6,885, ios-only 0 of 385, mac-only 0 of 219). Negative control: the same rows with their names shuffled flag 2,897, so the test can tell. That bounds any systematic slot shift: overload grouping, dropped thunk copies, virtual-base vftables set aside, and the D1+D0 merge show no off-by-one anywhere it could be measured.
- ICF-fold acceptance is weak, but I found no case where it is wrong. pair_names.py accepts several oracle names at one address when the string sets match, the callee counts match and the sizes are within 32 bytes or 2x. That test cannot tell `return 0` from `return 1`, or one field offset from another. tools/folded_body_check.py disassembles the oracle bodies behind the 332 folded addresses and finds normalized Mac bodies that differ at 29 addresses (iOS 145, mostly Thumb literal-pool noise). Every Mac case I read can still be one MSVC body. Some are template instantiations that differ only in a field offset or element type, such as PropDescriptor getValue for an enum, float, double and bool all returning through the same call+ret. Others are tail calls through a vtable (`mov eax,[ecx]; jmp [eax+X]`), which look the same whatever the arity, such as 0x00981920 GameTool::onMouseIdle(UIEvent const&) next to MemoryBinder::announceID(XmlNameValuePair const*, DescribedBase*). Tightening it means comparing each oracle body against the PLAYER body, not only the oracle bodies against each other.
- Naming defect, not a pairing defect. At a folded address the row order is arbitrary, and the evidence column is the union for the whole address, not the evidence for that row. Examples: 0x00575d00 (`ret 4`, 42 names) has BaseScript::extraErrorReporting(lua_State*) as its first row, although the sampled slot is GfxBinding::updateEntity(bool) / GfxPart::updateCoordinateFrame(bool). The row naming 0x00962a50 RBX::BuoyancyBallContact::getType() cites 'Ogre::RbxCullableSceneNode vftable 1 slot 10', which on iOS is GfxPart::getPartCount. Anything that pushes 'the first name per address' into ida/PLAYER.i64 will put unrelated aliases on trivial bodies. Those addresses should get a FOLDED/alias treatment, or the most-voted name.
- Convention, not an error: D0 rows put the Itanium deleting-destructor symbol on the MSVC scalar deleting destructor (??_G). All 6 sampled D0 rows (0x006f9f70 0x0070cb40 0x009745f0 0x0040e610 0x00698170 and the folded MultiJoint one) are the standard `call dtor; test [esp+4],1; operator delete; ret 4` shape, and the callee is the matching D1/D2 body.
- By-catch outside this lens (not counted above): 0x00743ce0 is proposed by callgraph as RBX::operator==(RBX::ContentId const&, RBX::ContentId const&). Its body is a TypedPropertyDescriptor getValue: `mov ecx,[ecx+0x28]; call [vtbl+8]; ret 8`, which is the getter called from TypedPropertyDescriptor<RbxRay>::getStringValue at 0x00700840. That name is wrong. The arity tool run with --cls callgraph or --cls string-unique flags 251 and 347 rows. Most are cdecl free functions, which the thiscall test does not cover, but member rows such as 0x0040a6a0 _Rb_tree::_M_erase(node*) with `ret 12` deserve a look under those lenses.
- Not determined: whether a trivial folded PLAYER body (ret / ret N / return const) is the 'right' one of its aliases. Under ICF it is all of them. The slot evidence settles which vftable slots point at it, not which source function the linker kept.
