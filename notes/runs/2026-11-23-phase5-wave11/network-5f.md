## Patterns proven

- **`instance->fastDynamicCast<T>()` (member form) vs `Instance::fastDynamicCast<T>(instance)` (static form)
  are different code**: the member form reads the descriptor at `[esi+0x28]` and returns `esi`; the static
  form converts to DescribedBase (`lea esi,[eax+0x1c]`, null test) and back (`lea eax,[esi-0x1c]`).
  Player::verifySetParent 0x00541ea0 is the member form (75% -> 100%); Players::askAddChild 0x0070a240
  (folded with PlayerGui::askSetParent) is the static form.
- **A `boost::scoped_ptr<T>` member with trivial T emits no ctor code but takes an EH state**: Player's ctor
  0x00550d40 was one state short until 0x128 became a scoped_ptr (the dtor's `operator delete` of it was the
  other tell).
- **`__declspec(noinline)` on a STUB body is a usable stand-in** while a callee's real body is unwritten: VC9
  otherwise inlines `STUB(addr)` bodies into same-TU callers (it shows as `mov [g_foldingDummyVariable], fn`).
  removeCharacter 0x00553b40 and onServiceProvider 0x00553a10 reach 100% with setCharacter,
  onCharacterDied, doPeriodicIdleCheck, onCharacterChangedFrontend stubbed this way. Remove the specifier
  when each real body lands.
- **An assert line far below the current file end is placed with blank lines** (as Server.cpp already does):
  registerLocalPlayerNotIdle sits at the end of Player.cpp with its assert on line 838 (100%).
- Player's `operator new(0x1d8)` caller and the Countable increment order above are the 2013 Player ctor shape.
