## Pattern proven

- **A same-TU STUB callee changes the caller's register allocation, not only its EH states.** VC9 knows the STUB
  clobbers no registers, so `ServerReplicator::dataOutStep` kept `this` in eax across the `noinline` expireItems
  STUB, where 2013 pushes esi. One cross-TU call in the stub body (`RBX::Time::now<RBX::Time::Fast>()`, which the real
  body also calls) gives 2013's prologue: 0x00576990 went from 85.7% to 100%. The same applies to every STUB that sits
  in the same TU as a written caller.
- **An inline getter can decide register assignment.** With `Player::getUserID` out of line the source compiled to
  2013's calls and stack slots, but with `this` in esi. Inline, `this` lands in ebp and every push matches. The
  out-of-line copy 2013 keeps (0x00527420) comes from the reflection descriptor's `&Player::getUserID`.
- RakNet 2013 has a Roblox-added `SystemAddress::GetBinaryAddress()` (iOS symbol; in PLAYER it is ICF-folded into
  0x008291e0 with `BitStream::GetNumberOfBitsAllocated`). It is added to `raknet/Source/RakNetTypes.*` right before
  GetPort, which is its iOS position.
