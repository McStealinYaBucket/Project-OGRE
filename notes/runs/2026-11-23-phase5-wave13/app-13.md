# Wave 13: App, the shell's headers, FastLog, member.h, converts (app-13)

- **Owner:** Client/App and Client/Base.
- **Build dirs:** build-ap/ and build-ap-studio/. Scripts, runs and snapshots are in build-ap/w/: `snap.sh`, `ab.sh`, `rounds.sh` (K regen rounds of 2 runs each, then one final run) and `cmp.py`.
- **Baseline:** sweep19, which has PLAYER 11,797 and STUDIO 11,197 entries at effective 100%.
- **How I measured:** I used a private root (build-ap/w/root), with CSVs regenerated by gen_reccmp_csv.py from pooled runs of the current link. The shared reccmp/ was not written.
- **Caveat:** the shared working tree also held the network and shell agents' uncommitted work (Network/Player*, Players*, Client*, GameVerbs, Win/placeholder), so their gains are in these totals too.
