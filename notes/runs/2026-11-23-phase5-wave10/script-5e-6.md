## What changed

1. **`rbx/boost.hpp`, 2013 `type_holder` is `{ const std::type_info* type; destruct; construct; }`**
   (12 bytes).
   - `typed_holder<T>()` stores `type` first, then `construct`, then `destruct` (+0, +8, +4; the
     inlined singleton in `operator=<bool>` 0x0044c630).
   - `placement_any::type()` returns `typeid(void)` when the holder is empty.
   - Two 2013 cast forms:
     - **Pointer `any_cast<T>(placement_any*)`:** address compare, then `type_info::operator==`.
       Out-of-line copies are 0x0079f730 and 0x006fdad0.
     - **Reference `any_cast<T&>(placement_any&)`:** address compare, then `type_info::operator!=`,
       then `boost::throw_exception(bad_placement_any_cast())` (0x00701140, 0x0058ef20).
   - **`bad_placement_any_cast : std::bad_cast`** with its own `what()`.
   - `holder`/`data` are public (the casts read them). The old const-ref friend `any_cast` that
     `withVariantValue` uses is kept.
2. **`reflection/Type.h`:**
   - `Type::tag` is `const Name&`, initialised with `*Name::lookup(name)` and `*Name::declare(tag)`.
     The asserts read `!this->tag.empty()` on 66/77/98.
   - `Variant::tryCast<T>()` is `rbx::any_cast<T>(&value)`. `cast<T>()` is `rbx::any_cast<T&>(value)`,
     and the const form uses `const_cast`, as the 2013 `get<T>() const` does.
   - `Variant::isType<T>()` is `&_type->type == &typeid(T) || typeid(T) == _type->type` (`get<ValueMap>`
     0x005bb850).
   - `genericConvert` passes `rbx::any_cast<std::string>(value)` by value (the 0x00533f90 call plus
     its EH frame) and tests `isType<std::string>()`.
   - Users updated: `LuaInstanceBridge.cpp` 404/802 (`tag.str`), `reflection_property.cpp`, and
     `XmlSerializer.cpp:537` (dropped the `*`).
3. **`Util/Name.h` and `util/Name.cpp`:** `Name::lookup(const char* const&)`. The NULL test comes
   first, then `lookup(std::string(name))`. Both lookups move after `getNullName`, in 2013 address
   order (0x00613ab0, 0x00613be0).
   - Still below 100: the four in item 2 of the blockers, all include spellings.
4. **`reflection/type.cpp`:**
   - Includes `reflection/type.h`: the 2013 assert path of the 4 `Type<T>` ctors in this `.obj`.
   - The Array/Dictionary converts call `rbx::any_cast<T>(&value)` directly and **do not update
     `_type`**, as in 2013 (0x007a01d0 stores only the value). Two comment lines keep asserts
     125–140 on their lines.
5. **`reflection_property.cpp`:**
   - Asserts moved to their 2013 lines: `~EnumDescriptor` 61, the PropertyDescriptor ctor 91 (the 92
     assert is still `type.name->str.empty()`; see blockers).
   - `allEnumsNameLookup()[&*name]`: 2013's key is a temporary, because `Descriptor::name` is a
     `const Name&` there.
6. **`ScriptContext::startScript`:**
   - The body from `int identity` through `lua_settop(globalState, -2)` sits in
     `try { } catch (...) { --reentrancy; throw; }`. That is the 2013 handler: 0x006871ad decrements
     +0x120 and rethrows.
   - This gives the EBP frame and `mov [ebp-0x10], esp`. Asserts 2549/2550/2621/2634/2776/2780 stay

## Patterns proven (for CLAUDE.md, the integrator's call)

- **2013 `rbx::placement_any` is boost::any-shaped.**
  - Holder: `{type_info*, destruct, construct}`.
  - `type()` returns `typeid(void)` when empty.
  - Every type test is the address compare first, then the imported `type_info::operator==` (the
    pointer cast and `Variant::isType`) or `operator!=` (the throwing reference cast).
  - Tell: `cmp ecx, offset ??_R0...; je` followed by `call [__imp_??8type_info]`.
  - It moved 800+ PLAYER functions.
- **The two operand orders tell the two expressions apart.**
  - `rbx::any_cast` gives `ecx = holder type; push typeid(T)`.
  - `Variant::isType` gives `ecx = typeid(T); push _type->type`.
- **`Variant::cast<T>` throws `bad_placement_any_cast` through the reference cast.** There is no
  "Variant cast failed" string in 2013.
- **2013 `convert<Array/Dictionary>` leaves `_type` stale after the empty-table swap.** 2016 added the
  store.
- **`Type::tag`, and almost certainly `Descriptor::name`, are `const Name&`.**
  - Tell: `map[&name]` builds a temporary key (`mov edx,[esi+0xc]; mov [esp+N],edx; lea eax,[esp+N]`).
  - Tell: the assert texts `.empty()` on a Name.

## What blocks the rest (owners outside my files)

1. **`Descriptor::name` as `const Name&`** (reflection_property.cpp:92 `!type.name.empty()`). This is
   tree-wide: every `->name->str` and `desc->name` use outside reflection/ and script/ has to change at
   once. It is the integrator's call. Once it lands, `[&*name]` in the EnumDescriptor ctor becomes
   `[&name]`.
2. **Include spellings of `reflection/Type.h`** in files I do not own. Each costs one `Type<T>` ctor
   its 100%:
   - `util/ProtectedString.cpp`: `reflection/Type.h`, for 0x0066ef90.
   - `util/ContentId.cpp`: `reflection/Type.h`, for 0x006fe1a0.
   - `v8tree/EnumProperty.cpp`: `Reflection/Type.h`, for 0x00793a90 `Type<Region3int16>`.
   - `Type<ValueMap>` 0x0079fba0 asserts `reflection/type.h`, but its `getSingleton` lives in
     EnumProperty.cpp:324. 2013 defined it in type.cpp (it sits among type.cpp's ctors 0x0079fa00–0x0079fc70).
     Moving that `getSingleton<shared_ptr<const ValueMap> >` into type.cpp is the v8tree owner's call.
3. **`Limits::Counter::canAdd` returns int** (`test eax,eax` at 0x0087af41; Base `rbx/Limits.h` or
   Util `Limits.h`). It blocks `ObjectBridge::newInstance` together with the EBP/EDI allocation.

   40–56%). They should now be rewritable on the `rbx::any_cast` forms above, the same way as

5. **The `get<T>` copies in the client shell** (`get<bool>` 0x0044aef0, 80%, `/GS` LTCG copy) differ
   only by register choice in the inlined Variant copy. Shell owner.
6. **startScript (48.7%)** still differs in its body. The `script` px stays in edi in 2013, but ours
   reloads `[ebp-0x1c]`. Also missing are a `"%s(%d) - %s"` `RBX::format` throw (0x0068701a) and the
   2013 ScriptStatInformation shape.
7. **callCallback 0x0087b940 (28.8%)** still sits in ThreadRef.cpp. Its 2013 assert lines (659–702) fall
   inside our `on_newindex` (551–807), so the move needs on_newindex shortened first. Not attempted.
8. **192 unnamed ScriptContext functions:** not reached this pass.
9. **Generator/pairing:**
   - `boost::throw_exception<std::exception>` 0x0044d400 is really
