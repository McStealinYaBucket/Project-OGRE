## Patterns proven

- **A bool setter compares the argument first** (`cmp al,[ecx+X]`): `if (value != member)`; `member != value`
  gives `cmp [ecx+X],al` (1 byte off). setUnder13 0x00527690, setHasGroupBuildTools 0x00527760.
- **`BoundProp<V>::setValue` inlines to `getset->setValue(this+0x1c, &v)`** through the static's `getset`
  member (`mov ecx,[desc+0x28]; call [vtbl+0xc]`): setSimulationRadius 0x0052e510.
- **`G3D::clamp(float,float,float)`** gives `comiss lo,v; jb` then `comiss v,hi; jb` (upstream's `<=`/`>=`
  form): setMaxSimulationRadius 0x0052e570.
- **A read-only property plus a REPLICATE_ONLY twin** (MembershipType/MembershipTypeReplicate,
  AccountAge/AccountAgeReplicate): the setter raises the Replicate descriptor first, then the UI one
  (setMembershipType 0x00527800, setAccountAge 0x00527830).
- **Include fallout:** a Network header that includes `V8DataModel/FriendService.h` makes Network TUs
  (linked before App) supply `FactoryProduct<FriendService>::Creator` with Network's `include\Util/Object.h`
  spelling; PLAYER getClassName 0x004200e0 (App's `include\util/object.h` in its assert string) dropped to
  92.59% until the include was removed (back to 100%; the ctor 0x00422ee0 is covered under Losses). Network headers must not include App service headers they only need an enum from.
