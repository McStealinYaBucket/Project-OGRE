## Pattern proven

- **VC9 places an overloaded virtual's vftable slot at the first declaration of its name, even a non-virtual one.**
  Declaring the non-virtual `Replicator::readChangedProperty(BitStream&)` before the virtual
  `readChangedProperty(BitStream&, Property)` (+0xc4) moved every Replicator slot from +0x78 on by 4: wantReplicate
  0x0059cc90 called findTargetPlayer through +0x8c and filterChangedProperty 0x00591f00 isLegalSendProperty through +0xb8.
  Declaring the non-virtual overload after the virtual block restored both. (Rule for every class with a virtual whose
  name is also used by a non-virtual member.)
