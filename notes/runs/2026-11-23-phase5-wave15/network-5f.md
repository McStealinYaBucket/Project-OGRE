## Patterns proven

- **A same-TU STUB is proven nothrow, and that drops an EH state in a ctor that calls it, even with
  `__declspec(noinline)`.** The ReplicatorStats ctor STUB inside Replicator.cpp cost the `mov byte [esp+0x48],3` before
  its call. Moving the STUB to its own TU (ReplicatorStats.cpp) restored it, and the ctor went from 94.75% to 100%.
- **The idiom for a ternary passed by reference to `formatValue<int>(const int&)`.** 2013's code (a store in each arm,
  then one shared lea/push/call, with the temp sharing the slot of the earlier Time::Interval temp) comes from a
  block-scoped local: `{ int senders; if (ps) senders = ps->numSenders(); else senders = 0;
  physicsSenders->formatValue(senders); }`. The ternary gives `xor eax,eax`. Two separate `formatValue` calls give two
  call sites. A function-scope local takes its own slot. PeerStatsItem::update 0x005ba060.
- **Implicit `DescriptorDictionary<T>` = Sender (out-of-line explicit specialization) + Receiver.** Replicator's ctor
  calls the sender ctor and zeroes the receiver's vector inline.
- **The SystemAddress dictionary keeps 2013's out-of-line split naturally.** `SenderDictionary<SystemAddress>` with an
  in-class ctor and an implicit `SharedDictionary<T>` ctor both stay out of line (0x0058f1c0, 0x00591720) without help.
- **CMake skips empty `.cpp` files at configure time** (`file(SIZE)` in `ogre_prior_sources`). After you fill an empty
  Network file, rerun `cmake -S . -B <dir>`, or its symbols stay unresolved.

## STUB with body written

- **`Server::onServiceProvider` 0x005b5310: 88.99%.** Every call, store and EH state matches. The original's frame is 8
  bytes larger (`sub esp,0x18` against our 0x10): its `shared_ptr<NetworkOwnerJob>` temp has its own slot, while ours
  shares the bind temps' slot, so every stack offset shifts by 8. A `DataModel* dataModel` local (2016 shape) does
  not change this. The remaining diffs are that offset shift plus the template calls that still need pairing.
